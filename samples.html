  • Procedural Spider Locomotion Epic’s Locomotor extended with custom foot placement and physics.

    I used Epic’s Locomotor plugin as the foundation for procedural spider locomotion. The Control Rig setup generates steps at runtime and adapts foot placement to uneven terrain.

    Epic’s ground probes were locked to world Z. I fixed the source to follow the rig’s orientation, so the spider adapts to any angle, including a full 180° inversion.

    Offset sets the ground probe’s direction and reach.

    LocomotorCore.cpp

    The spider adapts its footing as the support widens or narrows. I added inward sphere casts to Epic’s Locomotor to find side contacts when the ground probe misses.

    The spider’s reference automatically adjusts pitch and roll to nearby geometry. I derive its tilt from PlantNormal, the combined normal of surrounding sphere probes.

    I integrated Control Rig Physics after Locomotor and Full Body IK. Spring-damped bodies add inertia and world-collision response, while kinematic anchors preserve procedural control.
